\subsection{Non-degenerate case}
In the nondegenerate case, the spectral density function $\psi_{k,\epsilon}^{\iota}$ is defined by (2.6). Its higher-order spectral derivatives satisfy
\begin{equation*}
    \left(\partial_y^2-k^2-\frac{b''(y)}{b(y)-\lambda+i\iota\epsilon}\right)\left(\partial_\lambda^a D_\lambda^b \psi_{k,\epsilon}^{\iota}-\mathcal{S}_{a,b,\epsilon}^\iota\right)=\mathcal{R}^\iota_{a,b,\epsilon}(y,\lambda).
\end{equation*}
As in the proofs, we group the terms in $\mathcal{R}^\iota_{a,b,\epsilon}$ according to their singularity structure.

Throughout this subsection, we use the following shorthand notations:
\begin{equation*}
  \psi=\psi_{k,\epsilon}^\iota,\quad\ b=b(y),\quad\ \chi=\chi_{\ast,\delta_0/10}.
\end{equation*}
